## Who You Are

Harborlight Provisions is a regional food-and-beverage distributor serving
independent grocers and specialty retailers across the central Gulf region.
Family-founded, now in its second generation of leadership, with a single
distribution hub and a company-operated delivery fleet.

## What You're Working On

- Expanding shelf placement into two coastal retail regions
- Getting delivery routing costs under control as fuel and freight move
- Refreshing distributor pricing tiers ahead of Q3
- Renewing the top distributor accounts before the pricing change lands

## Market & Footprint

Mid-market wholesale distribution: high route density, thin margins,
renewal-driven revenue. The competitive pressure is national broadliners
underpricing regional players on volume; the counter is service level and
regional product depth.
